What's in the valley

A full year of seasons, with something to do every day and secrets to find at your own pace.

Farm

Grow

Over twenty seasonal crops, a greenhouse, fertilizer, sprinklers and an orchard with real Brazilian fruit: jabuticaba, mango, guava and orange.

Animals

Raise

Cows, chickens, sheep, goats, Trovão the horse, Paçoca the dog and Mingau the cat. Talk, brush, take them to pasture and watch the hearts grow.

Building

Build

Rafa the carpenter helps you rebuild the barn, coop, stable and a four-level house. Build fences and gates, and fix the old cart.

Friends

Belong

Twenty-plus villagers with their own schedules, birthdays and stories. Heart scenes, marriage and family.

Festivals

Celebrate

Festa Junina with square dancing and a fishing game, an egg hunt, horse races and summer fireworks. Mountain mysteries, folklore secrets and a deep mine.

Country life

And more

Fishing in rivers, lakes and the sea, winter ice, cooking, a thrift shop, a post office, a library and a field notebook to fill.

How it works
