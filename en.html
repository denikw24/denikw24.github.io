A 3D farming game set in the Brazilian countryside.

Coming soon to Google Play and Steam. The whole first spring is free.

About the game

Your grandfather's farm sat quiet for years. Now it's yours. Clear the weeds, fix up the house, till the soil and watch your first harvest sprout.

Grow

Over twenty seasonal crops, a greenhouse, sprinklers and an orchard with jabuticaba, mango, guava and orange.

Raise

Cows, chickens, sheep, goats, Trovão the horse, Paçoca the dog and Mingau the cat.

Build

Rafa the carpenter helps you rebuild the barn, coop, stable and house. Build fences and gates, and fix the old cart.

Belong

Twenty-plus villagers with their own schedules, birthdays and stories. Marriage and family.

Celebrate

Festa Junina, an egg hunt, horse races, mountain mysteries and folklore secrets.

And more

Fishing in rivers, lakes and the sea, cooking, a thrift shop, a post office, a library and a mine.

The first spring (30 in-game days) is free. After that, a one-time payment unlocks the full game and you keep the same farm. No ads.
